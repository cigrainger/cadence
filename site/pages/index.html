Knowing has more than one sound

Hear. Explain.
Play. Create.

Naming a chord and anticipating its sound are different skills. Cadence keeps them separate, so a good answer in one area doesn’t hide a difficulty in another.

  1. 01

    Hear it

    Imagine a phrase before playing. Sing intervals with solfège. Compare two ways a chord can resolve.

  2. 02

    Understand it

    Explore keys, modes and harmony. Add your own score analysis, including alternative readings.

  3. 03

    Find it

    Use MIDI piano input or a guitar fretboard. Slow down, isolate a difficulty, then return to the phrase.

  4. 04

    Make it yours

    Write an answer, change an ending or record a MIDI improvisation. Save it beside the music that inspired it.

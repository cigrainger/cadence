# Cadence identity

A C-shaped phrase surrounds three short staff lines and a warm resolution point. Forest green and warm paper keep the app quiet; terracotta marks a musical question or change.

`mark.json` is the editable geometric source. Run `swift Scripts/make_brand.swift` on macOS to regenerate the SVG, PNGs and ICNS. The script uses AppKit and iconutil; it does not require an image generator or a proprietary design file. Preserve the icon's clear space. Use the wordmark in the system's serif font; no font is distributed.

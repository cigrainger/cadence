CADENCE — YOUR PRACTICE ROOM

Cadence now opens in Practice. The sidebar groups your music separately from
reference and tools; the existing Command-1 through Command-8 shortcuts keep
their meanings. Command-3 returns to Practice home.

If you have the smooth-changes pathway, its next session is one click away.
An unfinished session resumes the same prompt, feedback and attempts. A pending
answer after a failed save is retained in memory; Continue session takes you
back to save or discard it. It is not marked saved until the write succeeds.
Without a pathway, the home offers an interval exercise and a route to courses.

Bring it back combines passage reviews from your music, guided MIDI reading,
rhythm/timed practice and chord changes. It uses the existing review schedule,
keeps new activities distinct, and excludes changed or paused piece pathways.
The exercises themselves continue to schedule concept and singing questions;
the home is not an exhaustive count of every due question. Adaptive voicing
reviews return through the opening check of your suggested session.

Hear & sing, Find the groove and Follow the changes open existing activities.
All exercises opens the full practice workspace. Make it yours opens your
pathway composition or a recent composition, or starts a piano phrase.

Inside a smooth-changes session, the stage strip shows where you are, not a
mastery score. The score and input/feedback stay beside each other. After a
checked answer, or when you deliberately reveal a solution, a diagram connects
the low, middle and high voices. Labels include octaves; a dashed line marks a
voice that stays put. Independent prompts keep the destination hidden in both
the notation and the diagram. The home illustrates a separate seventh-chord
change rather than an answer from the opening triad check.

The reading and notation workflows remain available in Library. These changes
use no AI credits and require no data-format migration. The visual system still
uses the light appearance. Physical FP-10, microphone and full VoiceOver testing
remain to be done; the automated checks use virtual MIDI and isolated fixtures.

Hear & sing now opens the resumable hear → imagine → sing → play pathway.
Find the groove opens the sampled band. The legacy interval and rhythm games
remain in All exercises. Ear phrase reviews are selected on opening a new
ear-first session; the Bring it back list remains passage reviews.

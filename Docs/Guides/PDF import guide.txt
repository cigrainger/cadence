CADENCE · MULTI-PAGE PDF TO AN EDITABLE SCORE

PRINTED CHORD SYMBOLS
New requests also read chord labels with bar, beat and staff. Supported labels
become editable symbols above the notation. Source & draft → Chord symbols
lets you correct them alongside the original. Unsupported labels are listed
with their position in the page's review notes; check these before marking
the page reviewed. The model is instructed to copy printed harmony, not infer
it from the notes. Previously imported pages are not automatically resubmitted.
Recognition accuracy still needs your review. See Chord symbols guide.txt.

CHOOSE PAGES
Choose Import PDF in Compose or Studies. Choose your PDF, then enter Pages
such as 29–32, 35. Individual numbers and ranges can be mixed; duplicates are
removed and pages are processed in file order. PDF file numbers can differ
from printed book numbers. The viewer's page box only navigates the original;
Use viewed page selects it for import. Choose up to 16 pages per score.

Choose Guitar or Piano. GPT-6.1 Sol with high reasoning is the default.
Argon high, Astra and Flash high remain available. This choice does not change
your AI coach model. Add your OpenRouter key in Settings → AI coach first.

Passage on each page is optional: use it for an exercise, a system, or the
first 4–8 complete bars. Blank requests the visible written music, up to 32
complete bars on each selected page. Pages become one score with up to 128
bars, one key and one meter. A page that cannot fit reports its own error;
choose a shorter passage or import it separately.

Guitar imports expect treble notation written an octave above sound. Cadence
converts it to sounding pitches and proposes linked tab in standard tuning
with no capo. Change these in Full editor if needed. Piano retains treble and
bass staves with up to two independent voices per staff. This does not arrange
arbitrary piano music for guitar or reproduce printed tab fingerings.

PROGRESS, STOP AND RESUME
Import sends one selected page per request, sequentially, through OpenRouter.
The entire PDF is copied locally and is never sent as a whole by this importer.
ZDR is required, provider fallbacks are disabled and PDF parsing must be native.
No external OCR fallback or automatic paid retry is used.

Each page shows Waiting, Reading, Ready to review, Reviewed, Needs retry,
Stopped, or Removed from score, plus its combined-score bar range when ready.
Elapsed time is shown for the active request, with a four-minute total limit.
Completed pages and attempts save immediately. A failed page does not discard
other results; the queue continues with the next selected page.

Stop import cancels the current request and keeps completed pages. Close the
window, then reopen Pages & retries from the imported score. Resume waiting
pages processes only waiting/stopped pages; Retry failed pages processes only
failed pages. Each row also has its own Retry button. There is no automatic
resume after an app restart. Work already sent may still be charged; reported
costs and attempts with unreported costs are shown separately for each page.

The score explicitly identifies missing pages. Missing pages contribute no
bars: completed pages play consecutively until the missing music is imported.
When a retry succeeds, its bars are inserted in book order and later bar
numbers shift. Other pages retain their notes and corrections.

RETRY A CORRECTED PAGE
Select a page in the list, change its passage or model if desired, then Retry.
Retrying a page that already has music creates a replacement to review. It
never silently overwrites corrections. Review replacement shows its notation
and tab beside the original; Apply replacement replaces only that page's bars.
Keep current page discards the proposal. A pending replacement is saved across
restarts. Applying a replacement clears affected review marks and the editor's
older undo history; it cannot be undone back through an import operation.

REVIEW AND CORRECT
Open combined score, or Review on a page row. Source page selects that page's
bars and the matching original. Moving between bars also follows the source.
The editable panel shows up to two bars from the same source page at a time.
The original supports zoom, Fit, arbitrary page navigation and Open PDF.
Your corrections affect the editable score; the original stays unchanged.

Click a note or tab number, or use event arrows. Correct letter, accidental,
octave, duration, ties, chord tones and guitar string/fret. Piano Hand and Voice
select independent lines. Up/down arrows change pitch by semitone; A–G replace
a note and R makes a rest. Longer durations consume following rests, refusing
to erase notes. Full editor opens all passage, expression, MIDI and export tools.
Play these bars auditions the result. Practise these bars creates a practice copy.

Page reviewed is your own checkpoint. Corrections clear the affected page's
mark; global changes such as key, meter, tempo or tuning clear every mark.
Inserting/deleting bars updates the source ranges. New bars belong to the
page containing the insertion point, or the last page when appended. A fully
deleted page remains in the page list and can be imported again.

Check pitches, accidentals, octaves, rhythms, voices and page joins. Ties and
slurs across pages need particular attention: independent page requests do not
infer connections to unseen pages. Import notes includes the model's warnings,
flagged gap rests and proposed-tab guidance. Review marks are not an automatic
accuracy grade. Repeat signs are read as written bars once, not expanded.

SAVE AND BACK UP
The source PDF is copied into ~/Library/Application Support/Cadence/PDF Sources/.
The score, page queue, source ranges, model, attempts, reported costs, warnings,
review marks and pending replacements are stored together in editor.json.
Identical originals with the same filename reuse one saved PDF. Older single-
page imports still open and gain page controls when Pages & retries is opened.

Export MusicXML for notation/tab, MIDI for playback, or .cadence-score for an
exact editable copy. MusicXML and MIDI do not include source PDFs, review state
or API keys. Cadence score exports retain local references; back up the entire
Cadence data folder to keep the original PDFs. The key stays in Keychain.
If an original is missing, the score is still editable; restore PDF Sources
from your backup to review it or retry.

CURRENT BOUNDARIES
Unlocked PDFs up to 100 MB and 1,000 pages; each extracted page under 8 MB.
Up to 16 selected pages, 32 complete bars per request, 128 bars in the combined
score, 100 editable scores. One key/meter per score; up to two voices per staff.
Supported: common/dotted rhythms, complete triplet groups, chords, rests,
full-chord ties, dynamics, staccato and accents. Unsupported structures are
reported: pickups/incomplete bars, changing key/meter, more voices, cross-staff
voices and other tuplet ratios. Slurs, ornaments, repeats and layout need manual
review. General lossless notation exchange is not established.

VERIFICATION
Core regression tests cover page range parsing, merge order, source mapping,
bar edits, isolated review marks, size/key/meter rejection, staged replacements
and restart recovery. Native checks exercise a three-page queue with a failure,
individual retry, persistence, explicit replacement, stopping and late results.
These multi-page checks use local fixtures and simulated responses; no model
credits were used for this update. Earlier real-provider comparisons are in
Transcription research.txt; their small examples do not establish scanned-book
accuracy or guarantee another page's latency.

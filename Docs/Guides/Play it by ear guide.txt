CADENCE — PLAY IT BY EAR

Learn → Your pathway → Make a song your own → Begin today’s session.
The connected session is in current source builds; the 0.36.1 download predates it.
The pathway card recommends due familiar recall, or transfer after independent
familiar recall. Before review is due it offers supported practice and explains why.
Start with an original example or a reviewed library passage. The full piece
stays in Library, and Your pathway takes you back to the course map.

Hear → imagine → find and refine → make an answer → return later.

1. Hear the phrase before revealing its written notes. Carry its shape silently;
   sing or hum it to yourself if useful. Imagination is not measured.
2. Piano: enable MIDI listening, release any previously held keys, then play
   one note at a time. Release the last key before Check my take. Pauses,
   articulation, dynamics and tempo are not graded. Reset, disconnection or
   leaving capture stops it; a fresh capture is a supported retry.
3. Guitar: choose the Mac microphone or USB interface in System Settings → Sound,
   then choose the channel carrying the guitar. Count in & listen records a
   bounded clean single-note phrase. Keep playing through the count-in tempo;
   capture ends at the phrase boundary. This checks exact sounding pitches and
   attack order only, without loopback calibration or a timing grade. Mac-mic
   acoustic and wired-interface accuracy still require hands-on validation.
4. Inspect a guitar take before keeping it. Dispute an uncertain match. Noise,
   missing/extra candidates, a truncated take or disputed pitches are inconclusive;
   no correctness grade or inferred weakness is recorded. Raw audio is not saved
   or uploaded. A waiting take stays with its daily session, including after a
   failed save or navigation; retry saving without recording it again.
5. After a conclusive miss, focus on a suggested source bar. A focused guitar
   retry uses a slower invitation. Reconnect the whole phrase afterwards.
   Focused success never becomes independent whole-phrase recall.
6. Replays, notation/tab, solfège, source views and coaching are available.
   They count as support before answering. Opening the coach sends nothing;
   an explicit request shares symbolic music and bounded observations only.
7. Write a short musical answer in Studio: borrow a rhythm, change the ending
   or answer its contour. The blank draft shares the source key, meter and chord
   symbols. Enter notes or use MIDI step entry. Listen to the complete answer
   and Save finished answer, then Back to today’s session → Finish today’s
   session. This short answer has its own stable identity and source link; it
   does not claim completion of the separate eight-bar arrangement chapter.
8. Independent returns appear in Learn → Practice & review after time away.
   Familiar recall starts without hearing the melody. Transfer uses another
   piece, heard once as the new stimulus; another replay counts as support.
   Both start no earlier than a full day after relevant exposure and honour the
   activity’s spaced due date. Early guided practice stays open. A correct early
   supported pass does not erase an earned spaced interval. Later reviews can
   finish after the take; writing another answer is optional.
   Leaving an independent session before keeping its take makes its resumed
   capture supported practice. A received pending take keeps its original status.

The map reads canonical saved observations. Hearing, explaining, playing and
creating remain separate; neither a coach’s prose nor an inconclusive capture
awards progress. Saving a composition records creative work, not artistic quality.
Changed music keeps historical work but cannot receive old-source credit. A
waiting earlier-version guitar take can be kept as archival evidence or discarded.

This initial version retains up to 32 daily sessions and 16 microphone takes per
reviewed phrase. Archive completed sessions on the pathway card to free session
slots; the selected session, unfinished work, journal and compositions stay.
Clear older detector details in a guitar session explicitly keeps the two newest
pitch-only takes. Older per-note detector details are removed after confirmation;
pitch/order results, inconclusive summaries and exposure/review cutoffs stay.
Calibrated timing takes are protected. No cleanup runs silently.
Piano needs MIDI for measured playing; guitar requires clean monophonic input.
Chords, distorted guitar, bends, vibrato and picking-hand mechanics are outside
this check. VoiceOver, actual permission dialogs and physical-device trials remain
separate from the automated virtual-MIDI, synthetic-audio and native control tests.

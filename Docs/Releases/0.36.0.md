# Cadence 0.36.0 — a place to make music

The first public preview of Cadence: a free, copyleft Mac studio connecting music theory, piano, guitar, your scores and creative practice.

Explore scales and chords, edit linked notation/tab, bring in MusicXML or reviewed AI drafts from PDF, practise with MIDI, and build source-linked lessons from EPUB/PDF chapters. Hearing, explaining, playing and creating are tracked separately. Ordinary practice works offline; optional AI uses your own OpenRouter account.

This release also introduces a new original identity, a task-based guide, organised source, regression checks, dependency inventory and a public release process. The old decorative sidebar card is replaced by help and truthful storage status. Playback has a consistent stop control.

## Downloads

- The app ZIP is labelled with its actual architecture. The initial tested binary is **Apple silicon (arm64), macOS 14+**.
- `Cadence-0.36.0-source.tar.gz` contains the matching application source, build scripts and pinned dependency source archives.
- `cadence.cdx.json` is the CycloneDX dependency inventory. `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains checksums for the assets.

The app is **ad hoc signed, not Apple notarised**. macOS may require its normal Privacy & Security review before opening. Do not disable Gatekeeper globally. You may build from source instead.

## Preview boundaries

AI transcription needs human review. MusicXML support is deliberately bounded and is not a universal lossless notation converter. Guitar microphone matching is monophonic. Physical MIDI latency and microphone behaviour depend on your setup. The release checks use virtual MIDI, original fixtures and offline audio rendering; they do not claim physical-device or full VoiceOver certification.

Your saved work stays in Cadence's Application Support folder; back it up before trying development versions. No private books, user library, recordings, API keys or paid model response logs are included.
